Print page 1 for up to 50 questions. Use both pages for 51–100 questions. Print at actual size (100%) on Letter paper and keep all square markers clear. The preview has Print, Share and Download PDF controls. On iPhone, Share opens the system sharing menu where Print is available. Back to grader returns to your current work.

Choose a previously uploaded class, or choose Upload new class roster at the end of the menu.

Use an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with eight-digit student IDs and names. Keep leading zeros. Manage classes lets you rename or delete a roster; deleting it preserves previous exams.

Fill one answer for every exam question and any extra credit. Leave unused questions blank.

Upload Sheet 1, plus Sheet 2 if your exam has more than 50 questions. The question count, answers and extra credit are read automatically. Missing or unclear key answers will be highlighted.

Choose a saved key, or upload a new key and give it a unique name.

Guest mode does not need a key name or saved library. End the session to start a different exam after adding student sheets.

Open the camera or select photos, then tap Grade sheets. You can add many photos before grading. Keep this screen open while the batch processes.

Photograph one flat sheet with a little background on each side. Avoid glare and keep the markers visible.

Two-page exams are paired by student ID and can be photographed in either order.

No roster is needed. A one-page sheet with an unreadable ID is graded as Unidentified student. Two-page exams need readable IDs for pairing.

The bubbled ID must match the selected class. Your exam saves on this device as you add photos.

Review is optional for clear results. Retake replaces a poor photo; Exclude keeps it out of grading; Delete removes it, with Undo available until another deletion, exam switch or reload.

Email graded sheets to individual students sends each matched student’s own graded PDF through the professor’s connected Gmail account. Connect Gmail when prompted, then confirm once. The app requests send-only Gmail access and basic account identity, without inbox-reading permission. Google access tokens remain in memory, never in backups. Google may ask you to reconnect after a reload or authorization expiry. This does not create an Exam Grader account or sync your workspace.

Missing, invalid or shared roster email addresses and incomplete grades are skipped. Older rosters need re-uploading with an Email column for a new exam. Email requires internet and an open screen. Accepted means Gmail accepted the message, not guaranteed inbox delivery. If a send result is uncertain, check Gmail Sent; the app will not automatically resend that sheet. Prior sending status is included in new exam backups. Clearing browser data or restoring an older backup can lose that history. No automatic background email is sent. The Google app needs initial developer setup; wider public availability can require Google verification.

Download Combined PDF contains the ready students’ graded sheets. Download Class CSV contains their scores. Incomplete or unclear results are left out until resolved.

Blank and multiple student marks earn zero. Correct extra-credit answers add points without a penalty for wrong answers. Final scores are capped at 100%.

More options lets you include or hide correct-answer letters.
